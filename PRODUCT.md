# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), Tailwind CSS v4, React Hook Form, Recharts, Framer Motion

## Users

- **Admins (Logged-in)**: Business owners or managers setting up and maintaining their platform.
- **End-users (Non-logged-in)**: Customers interacting with the multi-tenant products (e.g., scanning a QR code to view a menu, pay, or leave a review).

## Product Purpose

An all-in-one platform that helps admins create dynamic landing pages, digital menus, process payments, manage reviews, execute marketing, and view analytics.

## Positioning

A unified, multi-tenant operating system that brings landing pages, menus, payments, and marketing into a single platform, eliminating the need to stitch together fragmented tools.

## Operating Context

Admins interact with a robust management dashboard to build and monitor their surfaces. End-users interact with the published dynamic pages, typically on mobile devices while at a physical location.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multi-tenant product structure.
- QR code generation and management.
- Dynamic page and menu builder.
- Analytics and marketing integrations.
